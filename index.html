◷ Appointments

  • Strictly online booking only — walk-ins are not accepted.

£ Pricing & Deposits

  • A £10 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your slot, deducted from your final bill.

↻ Reschedules & Cancellations

  • Reschedule more than 48 hours before your appointment.
  • Changes within 48 hours result in loss of deposit; same-day reschedules aren't permitted.
  • No-shows require a new deposit to rebook.
  • No refunds on deposits, completed services, or personal preference — please raise any concerns during your appointment.
